# Escalation

## Ask for More Compute When

- preview controls are directionally sensible
- a shortlist or platform decision depends on stronger evidence
- pose stability or discrimination cannot be answered from one sample

## Ask for More Provenance When

- a reference peptide came from lore rather than a primary source
- a sequence was proxied or simplified
- labels changed across reruns

## Pause Instead of Pushing Through When

- three failures in a row suggest a systemic issue
- the environment changed from the last known-good manifest
- a large batch is not resumable
